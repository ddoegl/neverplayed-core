---
description: Read a handover ticket created by another agent and integrate its objectives into your current scope.
---

1. **Read Ticket:** Read the specified handover file from
   `.agents/handovers/<ticket-filename>`.
2. **Acknowledge:** Briefly summarize the core objectives you've just ingested
   to confirm understanding.
3. **Integrate:** State your immediate next steps to resolve the ticket, and ask
   the user for permission to begin execution.
