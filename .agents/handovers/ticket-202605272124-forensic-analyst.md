- **Core Findings & Argumentation:**
  1. **Markov Blanket Integrity:** An L1 Being (Occupant) cannot possess a "Realist" view of the whole system without violating its Markov Blanket. The Idealist Perspective is the true L1 Viewport.
  2. **Unification:** The "Realist Perspective" is no longer an L1 UI toggle. It has been redefined as **L2 Inhabitation**—the act of the Grounding Soul shifting its cognitive focus to inhabit the L2 Realm Being and looking through its interoceptive somatic viewport.
  3. **The Unattended Holon:** Crucially, when the Grounding Soul shifts its focus to L2 Inhabitation, its original L1 surrogate *does not instantly dissolve*. Because it is an autonomous holon, it is left behind as an unattended "husk" that coasts on its remaining attention span. The L2 Realm (and other occupants) continue to perceive this unattended L1 surrogate until it eventually falls asleep due to homeostatic boredom.
